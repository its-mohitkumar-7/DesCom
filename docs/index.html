What is DesCom?

DesCom (Destination Confirmation System) is an IoT-based destination monitoring system that uses QR codes and an ESP32-CAM to confirm when a person reaches a designated destination and notify a registered recipient through Telegram.

Benifits

The idea behind DesCom is to make destination confirmation simple and automatic. Instead of depending on a child having a smartphone or requiring a parent to contact the child, the system can automatically notify the parent when the child reaches the destination.

How does it work?

The process starts when the child shows their QR code to the ESP32-CAM. The device reads the QR code and sends the information through Wi-Fi to the connected system. The student's information is then checked, and if the QR code is valid, a Telegram bot sends an alert to the registered recipient.

Benefits

DesCom provides a simple way to receive a destination confirmation without requiring a smartphone from the child. It can provide quick notifications, reduce uncertainty for parents, and give them more time to take further action when necessary.

What will you build?

You will build a complete destination confirmation system using an ESP32-CAM, QR codes, Wi-Fi, a database, and a Telegram bot. The finished system will scan a student's QR code, verify the information, and send a confirmation notification to the registered recipient.

What will you learn?

While building DesCom, you will learn how different technologies can work together in a single project. You will work with ESP32-CAM, QR-code scanning, Wi-Fi communication, HTTP requests, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, and Telegram bot notifications.
